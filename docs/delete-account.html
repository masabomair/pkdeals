Delete your Sale Mela account

You can delete your account and all data stored with it at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Sale Mela and go to Profile.
  2. Tap ⋮ next to your name, then Delete my account.
  3. Confirm, and sign in once more when asked.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email our support address (being set up) from the email address of your account, with the subject Delete my Sale Mela account. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account (name, email, profile photo) and your sign-in.
  • Your saved items and the brands you turned alerts on for.
  • Your sign-up date, last visit and device type.

Visit counts in our analytics don't include your name or email and are removed automatically after 2 months (Google Analytics' default). Nothing else is kept.
