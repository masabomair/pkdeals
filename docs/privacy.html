Privacy Policy

Sale Mela · last updated 28 November 2026

Sale Mela shows discounted clothes from Pakistani brands' public websites. You can use it without an account. This page explains what we collect if you do sign in, and why.

If you don't sign in

  • Your saved items, brand alerts, theme and filter choices stay on your device only.
  • We count visits with Google Analytics for Firebase (pages and screens opened, device type, approximate country). This does not include your name or email.

If you sign in with Google or email

  • We store your name, email address, profile photo (Google only), sign-up date, last visit and whether you used the app or the website.
  • We store your saved items and the brands you turned alerts on for, so they appear on all your devices.
  • Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google). We never see your password.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with brands or advertisers.
  • We don't take payments. Purchases happen on each brand's own website, under that brand's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Delete your account any time in the app: Profile → ⋮ → Delete my account. Or see how to delete without the app. Signing out leaves your account in place but removes nothing.

Brands

Sale Mela is independent and not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any brand it shows. Brand names, logos and product photos belong to their owners; prices and stock come from each brand's public website. Brands that want to be removed can contact us.

Contact

Questions or requests: our support address (being set up).

Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Analytics): Firebase privacy
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and the deal data.
